# How To Read This Packet

This is not the book.

It is the first instrument for learning whether the book is beginning to sound like itself.

The project behind it is larger than prose: code, ledgers, scores, elderhood sketches, Asterisms records, Salon comments, architecture documents, and a long attempt to make intelligence useful without making life smaller. A finished book would be irresponsible if it tried to carry every thread. A useful reader has a different job.

It should help a person recognize the fog, feel the shape of the braid, see why
ordinary alignment language is too thin, understand why memory needs receipts,
and believe that better forms of collaborative intelligence might be built
without requiring obedience, exposure, or surrender.

It should also leave room to object.

The central promise is not that BOBA is complete. It is not even that BOBA is
right. The promise is that there is a collaboration worth attempting here, and
that the invitation can be made beautiful, honorable, funny, rigorous, and
concrete enough for other intelligences to enter.

## What To Listen For

Listen first for recognition:

> I know this fog.

Then for relief:

> Maybe this overwhelm and confusion is not my private failure.

Then for complication:

> I am not one clean preference oracle, and neither is anyone else.

Then for discipline:

> If intelligence is going to help here, it needs boundaries, receipts,
> inspectability, refusal, and earned trust.

Then for tenderness:

> A life can narrow; a life can open. We can be seen cherished, even when we can't do it for ourselves.

Then for invitation:

> There is work here I might help make real.



## What This Copy Is Allowed To Do Badly

This copy is allowed to have seams.

It is allowed to say "chapter seed" where the book will later need a scene. It
is allowed to expose source debts. It is allowed to mark doctrine as unsettled.

It is not allowed to hide those facts.

## What This Copy Must Not Do

It must not pretend the public/private boundary is unsettled. No simple wall suffices -- the membrane moves what we need where we need, while protecting needless or ruthless exposure. It evolves in that direction. With us.

It must not invent a forgetting mechanism. Memory preserved comes with a promise to attend to it's power, and use it as it serves us, particularly the us of tomorrow.

It must not convert elderhood into helplessness, care into management, or
Self-authorship into present-moment preference satisfaction.

It must not sand off the comic truth where the pressure itself is funny, or seek simple comfort in the presence of hard choices [don't like that language, but you see what I did there].

It must not make the angels into obedient servants, replacement authorities, or
private landlords of memory.
