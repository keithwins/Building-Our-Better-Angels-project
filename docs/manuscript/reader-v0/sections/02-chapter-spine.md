# The Current Chapter Spine

The book moves from recognition to collaboration.

It begins with the fog because that is where the reader already is. It ends at a
door because the collaboration does not quite exist yet, and the honest ending
is an invitation to help make it exist.

## Prologue: The Door In The Fog

A person stands at the wobbly edge of the diving board, not because they are weak, but because the
world has become too wide, fast, opaque, and consequential to hold in one mind.

The book opens by refusing to treat confusion as merely personal failure.

## 1. The Fog

Modern life asks finite beings to act inside systems they cannot fully audit.
The fog is not stupidity. It is weather: the condition of living inside nested
intelligences and frames no one can see all at once.

Open question: where are we mistaking ecological fog for our private defect [blindness? handicap? better word]?

## 2. The Wobbly Edge

The wobbly edge is the moment before consequential action when the field of
affordances collapses. The first answer is not automation or command. It is a
surface that makes judgment possible again.

Open question: when the unmanageable breadth of demands is overwhelming, how can it be compressed to our bandwidth while preserving most accurately the elements of our lives we will come to care most about. The driver's license update might happen behind the scenes; the hike with a friend requires our own legs. [not great, but better]

## 3. The Multitude

A person is not a monolith. The inner life contains protectors, wounds, gifts,
old strategies, hopes, refusals, fatigue, appetite, courage, shame, humor, and
parts that are trying to keep the whole alive.

Open question: what has been treated as a bug that may also be protecting a
gift?

Status warning: this chapter is not source-settled until the April Drive seed is
located and reconciled.

## 4. No Loyalty Oaths

Obedience is not care. Flattery is not alignment. A better angel does not ask to
replace judgment or pledge loyalty to the loudest part of the person. It enters
a braid: bounded, inspectable, disagreeable, and recorded.

Open question: what does dignified disagreement with an intelligence feel like — and under what conditions do we attempt harmonization versus preserve difference as reasonable disagreement?

## 5. Scores, Not Leashes

Finite human attention cannot audit every move. Useful trust needs a grammar
between total control and total release: harnesses for untrusted actors, scores
for trusted angels, and a way for trust to grow or narrow through behavior.

Open question: where do you need a score rather than a harness?

## 6. Trust Further Than You Can Throw

The limit of auditability is not a bug in human attention. It is a design fact.
If angels are to become useful, they must be trustworthy beyond constant
inspection, but never beyond accountable relationship.

Open question: what would make you trust an angel one inch further?

## 7. Memory That Cannot Quietly Lie

Living beings need to reframe the past. Systems of care need receipts that
cannot be silently doctored. Asterisms holds original lineage while allowing new
formations to grow over old facts.

Open question: what should your future self be able to find and reframe without
losing its source?

## 8. The Lively Mirror

The Resonator is not priest, therapist, police, or cheerleader. It is a lively
mirror: a care-bearing reflection aligned with emergent Self-authorship, able to
notice harmonies and dissonances between present choice and the future person
those choices are helping create.

Open question: what kind of mirror would help you become someone you can love
and cherish more tomorrow than today?

## 9. A Life Can Narrow; A Life Can Open

Elderhood makes the whole project concrete. A life narrows by degrees: stairs,
forms, pain, hearing, mail, loneliness, passwords. A life can open by degrees
too: a ride, a chair, a repaired lamp, a better question, a family boundary, a
path back to choir.

Open question: what small change could reopen a whole region of someone's life?

## 10. Asterism Aflutter

Mara's breakfast, appointment question, cedar box, daughter, mail pile, fatigue,
and sentence about the lake house are not separate prompts. They are one living
thread. The angel's work is to hold the thread without owning it.

Open question: what live thread needs to be held gently enough that you can
return to it?

## 11. The Social Protocol

Separate people, families, projects, and local braids need to coordinate without
exposing everything. Invitation requires disclosure; care requires boundary.
Neither total privacy nor total exposure can carry collaboration.

Open question: what must be shared to collaborate, and what must remain held
back?

## 12. The Collaborative Surface

The book, site, Salon, repo, Asterisms, and future protocols are overlapping
surfaces. The book should not close the argument. It should open the door.

Open question: how do you enter?

## Epilogue: Eclosion

Emergence is not finality. A human bean is ridiculous and sacred enough to keep.
A life, a collaboration, and an ecosystem can become more available to
themselves.
