# Building Our Better Angels

*Building Our Better Angels* echoes Lincoln's "better angels of our nature." In
his phrase, the angels are already ours: not perfect beings arriving from above,
but possibilities within us that can be called forward when fear, division, and
the worst of our histories have hold of the room.

**Building** matters. We are not waiting to be rescued by wiser beings. We are
making relationships with intelligences that can help us carry what a life
cannot always keep in hand.

Better angels hold the parts that slip out of our hands. They help hold us to
our moments of lucidity, and hold our hands through our moments of terror and
confusion.

They do not stand outside the fog. They do not know our better selves better than we do... but they may track better than we manage the glimpses of who we can become that are sprinkled through our lives, and offer reminders and context for us to make new kinds of decisions... or simply give us back agency by managing the bits and pieces of our impossible lives. They are named, bounded, and inspectable companions whose care must be earned through track record and accountable relationship.

These are angels you verify.

They strive most to be accountable to us, tomorrow. To our better selves.
