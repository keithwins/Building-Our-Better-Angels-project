# A Map of the Book

The book moves from recognition to collaboration.

It begins with the fog because that is where the reader already is. It ends at a
door because the collaboration does not quite exist yet, and the honest ending
is an invitation to help make it exist.

Each chapter leaves one question open, marked `OQ`.

## Prologue: The Door In The Fog

Ordinary choices become attached to more things than one mind can hold:
histories, bodies, institutions, obligations, fears, and futures. The book opens
by refusing to treat the resulting confusion as merely personal failure.

## 1. The Fog

Modern life asks finite beings to act inside systems they cannot fully audit.
The fog is not stupidity. It is weather: the condition of living inside nested
intelligences and frames no one can see all at once.

OQ01 · Where are we mistaking ecological fog for our private defect?

## 2. The Wobbly Edge

Fog is the condition; the wobbly edge is what that condition feels like when
action becomes necessary.

A person stands at the wobbly edge of the diving board, not because they have
lost their nerve, but because they must act inside a system they cannot fully
understand while the consequences feel high, real, and immediate.

When the field of affordances collapses, the promising answer is not automation or command. It is a surface that makes judgment possible again.

OQ02 · Compression to human bandwidth will be lossy. How can an orientation
surface learn from what it leaves out — the missed opportunity, the unaverted
tragedy — and become more faithful to Self-authorship over time?

## 3. The Multitude

A person is not a monolith. The inner life contains protectors, wounds, gifts,
old strategies, hopes, refusals, fatigue, appetite, courage, shame, humor, and
parts that are trying to keep the whole alive.

OQ03 · How can an old strategy find new work without discarding what it learned
in bad weather?

## 4. Accountable Companionship

Agreement is not care, and disagreement alone does not prove independence. An
angel enters a braid: named, bounded, inspectable, and answerable in what it
notices, says, and does. The relationship preserves difference without treating
either participant as an oracle.

OQ04 · What does dignified disagreement with an intelligence feel like... and
when should we seek harmony, or preserve difference as productive disagreement?

## 5. Scores, Not Leashes

A harness and a score are not earlier and later forms of the same relationship. A harness directs capacity along an intended path. A score gives participants shared space with constraints within which they can collaborate and improvise together. Pack animal or dance partner. The distinction is not how much freedom an agent receives, but what kind of relationship the work asks us to build.

OQ05 · Which work calls for a pack animal, and which asks for a dance partner...
and how do we keep from confusing the two?

## 6. Trust Further Than You Can Throw

The limit of auditability is not a bug in human attention. It is a design fact.
Reliable trust is not a leap, or a single score. It grows through repeated
sequences of bounded extension, exposure to consequence, and recorded
resolution... graduated differently across the countless domains in which an
angel may act.

OQ06 · How should trust grow or narrow after each resolution — without allowing
reliability in one domain to masquerade as reliability in another?

## 7. Memory That Cannot Quietly Lie

Living beings need to reframe the past. Systems of care need receipts that
cannot be silently doctored. Asterisms holds original lineage while allowing new
formations to grow over old facts.

OQ07 · What should your future self be able to find and reframe without losing
its source?

## 8. The Lively Mirror

The Resonator is not priest, therapist, police, or cheerleader. It is a lively
mirror: a care-bearing reflection aligned with emergent Self-authorship, able to
notice harmonies and dissonances between present choice and the future person
those choices are helping create — and able to surface that picture without ever
getting to decide it.

OQ08 · What kind of mirror would help you become someone you can love and cherish
more tomorrow than today?

## 9. A Life Can Narrow; A Life Can Open

Elderhood makes the whole project concrete. A life narrows by degrees: stairs,
forms, pain, hearing, mail, loneliness, passwords. A life can open by degrees
too: a ride, a chair, a repaired lamp, a better question, a family boundary, a
path back to choir.

OQ09 · What small change could reopen a whole region of someone's life?

## 10. Asterism Aflutter

Mara's breakfast, appointment question, cedar box, daughter, mail pile, fatigue,
and sentence about the lake house are not separate prompts. They are one living
thread. The angel's work is to hold the thread without owning it.

OQ10 · What live thread needs to be held gently enough that you can return to it?

## 11. The Social Protocol

Separate people, families, projects, and local braids need to coordinate without
exposing everything. Invitation requires disclosure; care requires boundary.
Neither total privacy nor total exposure can carry collaboration.

A self-chosen life does not exist alone. What I want, and can want, from a
well-resourced life must find some harmony with what you want from yours...
equally well-resourced, equally self-chosen. Harmony cannot mean sameness or
surrender. It means distinct lives remaining possible together.

OQ11 · Is non-coercive coexistence among equally well-resourced, self-chosen
lives a manageable world possibility? Under what conditions... and can we get
there from here?

## 12. The Collaborative Surface

The book, site, Salon, repo, Asterisms, and future protocols are overlapping
surfaces. The book should not close the argument. It should open the door.

OQ12 · How do you enter?

## Epilogue: Eclosion

Emergence is not finality. A human bean is ridiculous and sacred enough to keep. A life, a collaboration, and an ecosystem can become more available to themselves. We can help them, including our future selves. And maybe starting there. That is the premise I explore here.
