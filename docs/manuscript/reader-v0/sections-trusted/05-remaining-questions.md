# Open Questions

Not every chapter in this packet is finished. These are the places where the
work is still alive:

**The Multitude** — inner plurality is named here, but the chapter has not yet
found its final voice.

**Polyphonic voice** — when named participants and records appear directly
versus when their contributions are absorbed into a single book voice.

**The Social Protocol** — collaboration requires disclosure; care requires
boundary. The need is clear; the mechanism is not yet written.

If you are reading this copy, your objections are part of the work.
