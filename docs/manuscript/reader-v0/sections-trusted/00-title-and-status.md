# Building Our Better Angels

## An Invitation Reader

**Draft:** July 2026

This is an invitation, not a completed book.

It is a coherent door: a working voice, a chapter spine, and enough living
material to show where the collaboration is alive and where it is still finding
its footing.

## How To Read It

Read as an invitation, not a recruitment.

If a sentence sounds like it is trying to recruit, flatten, mystify, therapize,
or win the argument early, say so.

If a sentence helps a life become more available to itself, keep it alive.

Objections are part of the collaboration this packet is trying to open.
