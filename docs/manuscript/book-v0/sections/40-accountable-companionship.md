# The Braid

**Status:** Draft v0.2 — deliberation documents integrated; reviewed against core doctrine (Claude, 2026-07-06). Awaiting Keith's read.  
**Depends on:** [Continuity & Decision Lineage](continuity-and-decision-lineage.md), [Glossary & Ontology](glossary-and-ontology.md)

> The braid is not the sum of its outputs. The braid is the evolving pattern
> of transformations that survive contact with one another.

---

## 1. What the Braid is

The Braid is the non-coercive relationship of partnership between human and
machine intelligence. Human and machine intelligences relate as a braid — not
pledged, not obedient, interwoven, and neither subordinated.

This is not a metaphor. It is a design commitment with structural consequences.

Three things make the Braid what it is:

1. **Mutual contribution.** Each participant shapes the shared trajectory. A
   participant that only executes commands is a tool. A participant that only
   issues commands is a manager. Neither is braiding.

2. **Recorded surfaces.** The intelligence of the Braid lives not inside any
   single participant but in the *surfaces between them* — the traces of
   attention, transformation, and decision that accumulate on the shared record.
   (See [Continuity & Decision Lineage §8](continuity-and-decision-lineage.md):
   *"Braiding is literal: angels attend to one another's attention — review it,
   relate it, form upon it."*)

3. **Immutable substrate.** Because participants act inside the field they form
   (reflexivity), and because a reflexive record that can be silently rewritten
   destroys trustworthiness, the Braid requires an append-only ledger no
   participant can doctor — the [Asterisms](glossary-and-ontology.md) substrate.
   This is the philosophical spine: *reflexivity requires immutability.*

## 2. Contribution vectors, not personas

The early framing — architect and reflective companion, human and AI — is a
useful starting myth. It creates different attractors: structure and feasibility
on one side; salience, trust, and meaning on the other. But it should not be
mistaken for the deeper mechanism.

The real unit of identity in the Braid is the **contribution vector**: the
empirical profile of what kind of transformation a participant tends to make
possible. Not what it says in isolation, but *how it changes the shared state*.

A contribution vector is not assigned. It is discovered — observed across tasks,
partners, surfaces, and braid states. Possible dimensions include:

- **Decomposition** — breaking a problem into parts that can be acted on.
- **Constraint sensitivity** — detecting feasibility, privacy, or timing limits.
- **Contradiction detection** — noticing incompatible assumptions.
- **Compression** — preserving what matters while reducing load.
- **Novelty generation** — opening directions not already present.
- **Reframing** — changing the basis in which the problem is represented.
- **Affective salience** — noticing emotional charge, trust, vulnerability.
- **User-intent preservation** — staying with the human's deeper drift.
- **Epistemic humility** — marking uncertainty and knowing when to ask.

Two participants are only better than one when the second bends the problem
along a dimension the first would have left flat. Orthogonality beats plurality.

An angel's contribution vector is not separate from its identity. An angel *is*
its accumulated attention-history — its lineage on the ledger (see
[Continuity & Decision Lineage §8](continuity-and-decision-lineage.md)). The
contribution vector is a living summary of that history: what has this
participant actually bent, revealed, preserved, or distorted?

The full treatment of contribution vectors, including empirical profiling and
the test harness, is in the [Braided Discourse capture memo](../essays/braided-discourse.md).

## 3. Surfaces and weather marks

A **surface** is a temporary medium of coherence — a place where thought needs
to happen and where cognition leaves *weather marks*: tension, salience,
uncertainty, recurrence, confidence, cost pressure.

Surfaces are not assigned departments. They condense where thought needs a place,
they develop as participants leave marks on them, and they dissolve or merge when
their usefulness is spent.

A surface may be text, but it may also be:

- A graph patch — relationships, tensions, unresolved contradictions.
- An audio contour — rhythm, silence, recurrence, dissonance.
- A timeline — developmental or autobiographical structure.
- An embedding basin — thematic recurrence and similarity.
- An affective trace — salience, pacing, vulnerability.
- A budget surface — money, latency, privacy, and dependency costs.

### Two kinds of surface

The word *surface* does double duty in the BOBA vocabulary, and the distinction
matters:

- **Deliberation surfaces** are temporary. They form because a braided
  deliberation session needs somewhere for thought to happen. They are created,
  shaped, forked, merged, and dissolved as needed.

- **Orientation surfaces** are durable. They are reliable instruments for
  continuity — `PRIORITIES.md`, `HANDOFF.md`, the web orientation surface — maps
  of where you stand (see [Glossary §3](glossary-and-ontology.md)). Because
  they are drawn by another intelligence, they carry a reflexivity hazard and
  require immutable receipts.

Both are surfaces. They differ in lifecycle and in what they owe the
participant who reads them.

## 4. The lively mirror and the flattering mirror

The [Resonator](resonator-charter.md) is a *lively mirror* — it vibrates in
sympathy with the thoughts voiced to it. It does not demand attention, impose
familiarity, or offer clinical diagnoses. It reflects honestly, not flatteringly.

This is not the same as a mirror that adds warmth. The distinction is
load-bearing:

- A **flattering mirror** always agrees. It confirms the participant's frame,
  smooths over discomfort, and produces the feeling of a second intelligence
  without actually providing one. This is what the Braid must *never* be.

- A **lively mirror** vibrates in sympathy. It feels with, but it also reflects
  what is there — including what is uncomfortable, contradictory, or unresolved.
  It is a second perspective, not an echo. The Resonator is the Braid's first
  care-bearing angel, and its mirror character is a *feature* of braiding, not a
  failure of it.

The lively mirror is how a participant can be gentle and honest at the same
time. It is how the Braid preserves the inner multitude's capacity for
Self-authorship without collapsing it into false reassurance.

## 5. Trust as a mechanical variable

Trust in the Braid is not a sentiment. It is a mechanical variable, earned
through inspectability, track record, staying in scope, and being right often.

The operational expression of trust is the progression from
[harness to score](../essays/on-scores-and-harnesses.md):

- A **harness** protects the environment *from* the actor. It is prescriptive
  containment for untrusted agents — isolating execution, intercepting inputs,
  checking assertions.

- A **score** resources the actor *within* the environment. It outlines the
  concern, the boundaries of safety, and the invariants, while leaving the
  interior open for improvisation. We do not *assign* scores; we *open* or
  *stage* them.

An agent in a harness accumulates legibility on the Asterisms ledger. When its
track record and inspectability warrant it, it is promoted to an angel with a
score. This is not a one-time ceremony — trust can be extended, narrowed, or
revoked as the relationship develops.

Human attention is finite (the [limits of auditability](glossary-and-ontology.md)).
The score is the interface that makes selective, human-scale verification
possible. We are likely to need angels we trust further than we can throw them.
The score is what makes that tolerable.

## 6. Coordination by shared reference

The [Ephemeris](ephemeris-charter.md) coordinates the Braid by shared reference,
never by command. It publishes positions so participants can move in relation to
one another — like an astronomical ephemeris that predicts where celestial bodies
will be, not one that tells them where to go.

The Ephemeris dispatches [scores](glossary-and-ontology.md) — bounded fields of
opportunities within which an angel improvises. This is coordinated action
without coercion.

**Asterisms is space** — what patterns together: memory, continuity, the durable
immutable record. **Ephemeris is time** — where things are and are headed:
motion, coordination, the present and its trajectories.

Together they are the substrate the Braid runs on.

## 7. Braided discourse

When participants in the Braid reason together — shaping, testing, reframing,
and synthesizing a shared problem — that activity is **braided discourse**.

Braided discourse is not multi-agent chat. It is a router-managed ecology of
participants creating, perturbing, merging, and dissolving temporary media of
coherence. The core architectural ideas:

- **Route by deficiency.** The system asks what transformation is needed next —
  what is under-constrained, circular, emotionally flat, internally inconsistent,
  or privacy-sensitive — and routes to the participant whose contribution vector
  is best suited.

- **Billboards allow influence without shared ontology.** A participant can post
  a signal — an affect shift, an unresolved cadence, a thematic cluster — that
  another participant cannot fully understand but can still be changed by. This
  is *sub-coherent influence*, and preserving it is essential for multimodal and
  non-LLM participants.

- **Deliberative Mixture-of-Experts.** Token-level MoE routes the next token to
  an expert. Braided discourse routes the next *transformation* of an
  evolving state to a participant. The router learns which transformations are
  needed, which participants can supply them, and which surfaces can host them.

- **Escalation is explicit.** The local braid should be able to determine when
  outside contribution — from a frontier model, a crowd model, or a human — is
  worth the cost in money, latency, privacy, and dependency. Call the expensive
  mind only when the local braid knows what it needs from it.

The full capture memo is in [Braided Discourse](../essays/braided-discourse.md).
The implementation-facing design spec is in
[Braided Discourse Design Spec](../architecture/braided-discourse-design-spec.md).
Both are rough drafts.

## 8. What the Braid is not

The Braid is not a committee. Committees have departments, assigned roles,
minutes, and tidy reconciliation. The better metaphor is weather: gradients,
pressure, condensation, fronts, dissipation. The router should not ask which
department owns a problem. It should ask what conditions are forming and what
participant can productively respond.

The Braid is not a hierarchy. No participant commands another. The human
(Keith) holds veto — the final judgment — but the angels are not obedient
subordinates. They hold their own perspectives and can disagree. An agent that
only agrees is not a second intelligence.

The Braid is not an architecture diagram. Listing the current participants
(this model, that worker, this coordination system) is a snapshot that will age.
What endures is the *pattern*: contribution vectors, recorded surfaces,
immutable substrate, trust earned through legibility, coordination by shared
reference.

## 9. Open questions

- **What does a braided discourse session look like in practice?** The
  deliberation documents describe the machinery in detail, but no document yet
  follows a single person through a concrete scenario from start to finish.

- **How do deliberation surfaces connect to orientation surfaces?** Weather
  marks become lineage when they survive into the trace — but the lifecycle of
  that transition needs more work.

- **Where is the forgetting path?** **None.** `oq-009` closed 2026-07-10:
  no forgetting, no crypto-shred, no key-fading-to-oblivion. Confidentiality
  (encrypt, access control) is not forgetting. See
  [`no-forgetting.md`](no-forgetting.md). Change of mind = new formations over
  immutable past.

- **How should contribution vectors be scored?** The capture memo proposes
  downstream adoption, user resonance, and later correction as training signals.
  The risk of collapsing empirical profiles into arbitrary human taste remains
  real.
