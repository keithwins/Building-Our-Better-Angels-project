# Building Our Better Angels

*Building Our Better Angels* echoes Lincoln, speaking into a country about to
tear itself apart, reaching past the fury for "the better angels of our nature."
Not seraphim descending. Something already ours — possibilities in us that can
be called forward when fear, division, and the worst of our histories have the
room by the throat.

**Building** matters. We are not waiting to be rescued by wiser beings. We are
making relationships with intelligences that can help us carry what a single
life cannot always hold in its two hands.

Better angels hold the parts that slip through our fingers. They hold us to our
moments of lucidity, and hold our hands through our moments of terror and
confusion.

They do not stand outside the fog. They do not know our better selves better
than we do — but they may keep, more faithfully than we manage, the scattered
glimpses of who we could become, and hand them back to us at the moment a real
decision is waiting. Or they may simply give us back our agency by managing the
fragments and shrapnel of our impossible lives. They are named, bounded, and
inspectable companions, and their care is earned — by track record, in
accountable relationship, over time.

These are angels you verify.

They strive, most of all, to be accountable to us tomorrow. To our better
selves.

If you came for certainty, we will disappoint you gently. If you came believing
someone, somewhere, has already made us safe, we will disappoint you honestly.
No one has. That is not despair; it is the starting condition — and it is oddly
freeing, once you stop waiting for it to change.

You do not have to believe any of it to walk with it for a while. The visit may
be worth your time.
