# BOBA Core: Continuity of Judgment & the Asterism of Angels

**Status:** Adopted 2026-07-03. Canonical core doctrine.
**Folds in (supersedes):** earlier drafts `asterism-of-angels.md` (Claude) and
`resource-and-decision-continuity-model.md` (Codex) — two halves of the same
argument, now one document. Stub files removed 2026-07-10; full text remains in
git history.
**Audience:** Keith, future Keith, the angels, cold-reading agents.

> Two drafts converged from opposite directions and turned out to describe one
> thing: the *loop* BOBA supports — continuity of judgment across a whole life —
> and the *substrate* that makes that loop trustworthy — a reflexive record no
> participant can silently rewrite. §1–§5 are the loop; §6–§8 are the substrate;
> §5 is the hinge that joins them.

---

## 1. The subject is a living resource system, not a task owner

BOBA does not model Keith as an inbox with a person attached. It models a **living
resource system with a history**: attention, energy, body state, house and tools,
accounts and credentials, projects and obligations, relationships and privacy
boundaries, documents, stated priorities, unresolved decisions, and the record of
what has already been tried. Not all of it must be tracked perfectly. The point is
that it belongs to *one continuing life*, and BOBA's job is to help keep that life
legible to itself.

## 2. The job is continuity, not nagging

BOBA is not meant to flood Keith with tasks. Its job is to preserve what matters,
surface relevant context at the right time, notice drift between stated priorities
and current action, reduce unnecessary actuation burden, treat health/rest/
sustainability as first-class constraints, keep decisions recoverable, and keep
commitments visible without turning them into shame machinery. Support for
judgment — not a replacement for it.

## 3. Tasks are subordinate to decisions

A task is only meaningful inside a decision. "Fix X" presumes the decision that X
matters now; "read Y," that Y is the right input. So BOBA tracks the higher-order
facts — decisions, open questions, commitments, constraints, current projects,
energy and health state — and tasks become their mechanical expressions. This is
why the priorities instrument stores the *inputs to a judgment*, not a frozen rank
(see `private/PRIORITIES.md`).

## 4. The continuity loop

Observe current state → compare against commitments and priorities → surface the
smallest useful next step → check its fit with health, context, and consent →
record what was decided and why → preserve it so future sessions can recover it. A
life is not a one-shot optimization; it is continuous coordination under changing
conditions.

## 5. Decision lineage — "continuity with receipts"  *(the hinge)*

Each important decision leaves a trace: what was decided, what supported it, what
constraints were active, what remains open, what would cause revision. Future
decisions then refer back to real history instead of improvising a new self each
time. This is consistency tracking — not moral surveillance, not productivity
theater — **continuity with receipts.**

And "with receipts" is not a figure of speech. It is a requirement about the
*record itself* — which the next three sections make precise.

## 6. The record is an asterism of angels

BOBA's agents are an **asterism of angels**, not a flock. A flock is homogeneous
and herd-moving; an asterism is a chosen, revisable, human-projected pattern —
fitting for genuinely-other agents that take no loyalty oaths.

Read it as **both**: angels are the *attention* that constitutes asterisms
(observer-constituted fields of attention) *and* participants *within* them. The
observer stands inside the field it forms. When an angel decides or attends, that
act becomes itself a recorded material the next attention can see and connect to —
the record loops back to include its own author. (An asterism is
observer-constituted: the Big Dipper is not out there among the stars; the looking
makes it.)

## 7. Reflexivity requires immutability

That self-inclusion is **reflexivity**: the system records its own acts *inside
itself*; the decider is inside the record it keeps. This is exactly what "decision
lineage with receipts" (§5) demands — the receipts must include the angels' own
hands.

But reflexivity carries a hazard: if the party whose acts are recorded can also
*edit* the record, it can silently rewrite its own past — bury a mistake, invent a
prior decision, launder itself into false consistency. Therefore:

**Reflexivity requires immutability.** This is one-directional — an immutable
record need not be reflexive, but a reflexive one *must* be immutable to be
trustworthy. The record of an act must be append-only, unrewritable even by the
angel who authored it. That is precisely the Asterisms ledger's non-negotiable
property (see the registry-and-lineage contract): the store the brain cannot
silently rewrite. Immutability is what lets an angel act *inside* the field
without being able to doctor its own history.

So the loop (§1–§5) and the substrate (§6–§7) are one thing: **BOBA keeps a life
legible to itself by recording decisions and attention on a reflexive, immutable
ledger.**

## 8. What follows

- **An angel's identity is its accumulated attention-history** — its lineage. It
  becomes who it is by the durable trail of what it has attended to and formed.
- **Braiding is literal:** angels attend to one another's attention — review it,
  relate it, form upon it. The intelligence lives in the recorded surfaces between
  them.
- **One ledger, not two:** angels are materials-with-an-actor-role in the same
  Asterisms field — the same "don't split lineage" principle applied to Porter.

## 9. What the system must not become

BOBA must not demand constant manual triage, turn every ambiguity into a question,
convert temporary low energy into a failure narrative, force a single tasking
style, collapse life into a rigid checklist, or confuse throughput with a good
life. If it overwhelms discernment, it has failed. Attention is scarce; protect it
by batching non-urgent questions, using defaults already implied by prior
decisions, surfacing only the next few meaningful options, and not re-prompting
the same unresolved issue. This is how BOBA helps with executive load without
pretending the answer is "just be more organized."

## 10. The surfaces

This model is not a heavy process artifact; it is a small set of reliable surfaces
for continuity:

- **`private/PRIORITIES.md`** — the lightweight, live view of active projects,
  decisions, and constraints (the recompute-a-view instrument).
- **`HANDOFF.md`** — decision lineage in practice: a living, graded record so a
  fresh session, or a returning Keith, recovers state instead of re-deriving it.
- **`../architecture/orientation-surfaces.md`** — how to *enter and traverse* this
  corpus without reading everything (situated sensemaking).
- **The Asterisms ledger** — the durable, immutable substrate under all of it.
