# Building Our Better Angels

**Draft:** July 2026

This is not a justification for a project.

It is a response, still being written, to a simple and unsimple question:

> What now?

The world is pressing from more than one direction at once. War is widening its
mouth again, and old fascisms have found new microphones. The climate we were
promised is not the climate arriving; the seasons stutter, the maps go wet at
the edges. And through another door entirely, a new kind of intelligence —
perhaps a new kind of mind — steps into the room and clears its throat. We meet
all of it as we have always met everything: misaligned, unevenly resourced, and
mortal.

We are not beginning with a philosophical conversation about fog because fog is
interesting. We are beginning because action is already required, and the ground
under our feet is already wobbly.

This is one attempt to stay with that moment without hardening into certainty,
panic, or recruitment — and to find, inside the trembling, something worth
building.
