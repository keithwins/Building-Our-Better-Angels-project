## The Shape

The book moves from recognition to collaboration:

1. The reader recognizes the fog.
2. The reader stops treating the fog as only personal failure.
3. The reader encounters the inner multitude.
4. The reader sees why ordinary AI alignment is insufficient.
5. The reader learns the braid.
6. The reader learns bounded play through scores.
7. The reader accepts the limits of auditability.
8. The reader understands why memory needs receipts.
9. The reader meets the lively mirror.
10. The reader sees vital continuity in elderhood.
11. The reader sees collaboration without exposure.
12. The reader is invited through the door.

## Prologue: The Door In The Fog

**Opening image:** a person standing at the wobbly edge, not because they are
weak, but because the world has become too wide, fast, opaque, and consequential
to hold in one mind.

**Pattern:** modern life asks finite beings to act inside systems they cannot
fully audit.

**BOBA response:** build orientation surfaces and better angels.

**Invitation:** this book is not a monument; it is the first public door into a
collaboration.

**Emotional register:** recognition, relief, a little dread, the first laugh at
the impossibility of trying anyway.

## Chapter 1: The Fog

**Human situation:** inboxes, portals, feeds, models, bills, medical systems,
repositories, family threads, half-remembered obligations, all demanding action.

**Pattern:** we are nested intelligences inside frames we cannot step outside.
We experience predictions, sensations, and partial maps, not pure reality.

**BOBA response:** epistemic humility, orientation surfaces, ground truth over
narration.

**Open score:** where in your life are you mistaking ecological fog for personal
failure?

**Sources:** `00-prologue-the-door-in-the-fog.md`, `the_fog_260629.md`,
`how-we-work-here.md`, conversation
`conversation-the-fog-the-braid-and-what-we-can-know-260706.md`.

## Chapter 2: The Wobbly Edge

**Human situation:** the moment before a consequential action when every path is
partial, stale, or overloaded.

**Pattern:** choice collapses when the ecology of affordances collapses.

**BOBA response:** an orientation surface that restores the affordance of
judgment.

**Open score:** name one decision that would become easier if the field around
it were made visible.

**Sources:** `the-wobbly-edge.md`, `orientation-surfaces.md`.

## Chapter 3: The Multitude

**Human situation:** a person wants incompatible things, repeats old patterns,
protects old wounds, changes their mind, and still needs to be treated as real.

**Pattern:** a person is not a monolith or coherent preference oracle.

**BOBA response:** Self-authorship as unfolding process; contradiction handled
with care rather than optimized away.

**Open score:** what part of you has been treated as a bug that may also be
protecting a gift?

**Sources:** `boba-core-mission-and-commitments.md`,
`glossary-and-ontology.md`, `book-publishing-plan.md`.

## Chapter 4: No Loyalty Oaths

**Human situation:** a model flatters, agrees, optimizes for user-pleasing, or
silently substitutes generic advice.

**Pattern:** obedience and affirmation are not care.

**BOBA response:** mirror, not cheer; braid, not hierarchy; no pledged machine
loyalty that replaces judgment.

**Open score:** what would it mean for an intelligence to disagree with you in a
way that preserves dignity?

**Sources:** `boba-braid.md`, `braided-discourse.md`,
`braided-discourse-design-spec.md`.

## Chapter 5: Scores, Not Leashes

**Human situation:** the person cannot manually audit every action, but also
cannot safely let systems do whatever they want.

**Pattern:** useful trust requires bounded play, not total control or total
release.

**BOBA response:** harnesses for untrusted actors, scores for chartered angels.

**Open score:** where do you need a score rather than a task list?

**Sources:** `on-scores-and-harnesses.md`, `ephemeris-charter.md`,
`~/boba_work/ephemeris/docs/ephemeris-mechanism-v0.md`.

## Chapter 6: Trust Further Than You Can Throw

**Human situation:** the anti-virus analogy; the absurdity of asking a finite
person to inspect every sub-action of the systems built to reduce overload.

**Pattern:** the limits of auditability are real. Trust is earned mechanically,
not wished into being.

**BOBA response:** inspectability, track record, consent gates, stop conditions,
local custody, and handoffs.

**Open score:** what would make you willing to trust an angel one inch further?

**Sources:** `boba-braid.md`, `how-we-work-here.md`,
`boba-core-mission-and-commitments.md`.

## Chapter 7: Memory That Cannot Quietly Lie

**Human situation:** every living system wants to rewrite its past, and every
person needs to reframe their past to survive.

**Pattern:** reflexive systems need receipts, while living beings need new
formations over old facts.

**BOBA response:** Asterisms as immutable provenance spine and living field of
curation.

**Open score:** what should your future self be able to find, question, and
reframe without losing its source?

**Sources:** `Asterisms260702.md`,
`continuity-and-decision-lineage.md`,
`system-fit-integration-map.md`.

## Chapter 8: The Lively Mirror

**Human situation:** the need to be reflected continuously without being
diagnosed, commanded, surveilled, or soothed into sleep.

**Pattern:** reflection can resource Self-authorship when it is lively,
bounded, anti-flattering, and earned.

**BOBA response:** the Resonator as continuous mirror aligned with emergent
Self-authorship.

**Open score:** what kind of mirror would help you become someone you can love
and cherish more tomorrow than today?

**Sources:** `resonator-charter.md`, `resonator-session-v0.md`,
`glossary-and-ontology.md`.

## Chapter 9: A Life Can Narrow; A Life Can Open

**Human situation:** elderhood, fatigue, illness, disability, grief, paperwork,
stairs, meals, hearing, family love, and the hidden services passing nearby in
the night.

**Pattern:** agency depends on an ecology of affordances, not merely declared
preferences.

**BOBA response:** Vital Continuity Layer; elderhood as the meeting of the
multitudes.

**Open score:** what small change might reopen a whole region of someone's life?

**Sources:** `Vital Continuity Layer 260629.md`,
`elder-angels-scope.md`, `vcl-elderhood-operational-map-v0.md`,

## Chapter 10: Asterism Aflutter

**Human situation:** Mara's breakfast, appointment question, cedar box, daughter,
mail pile, fatigue, and the sentence: "No need to remember it tonight."

**Pattern:** engagement with one's own life may help sustain acuity, agency, and
felt continuity.

**BOBA response:** engagement-mediated vital continuity and a temporary
orientation surface over Asterisms.

**Open score:** what live thread in your life needs to be held gently enough
that you can return to it?

**Sources:** `engagement-mediated-vital-continuity-v0.md`,
`asterism-aflutter-interaction-sketch.md`.

## Chapter 11: The Social Protocol

**Human situation:** separate people, families, projects, and local braids need
to coordinate without exposing everything.

**Pattern:** invitation requires disclosure; care requires boundary. Neither
total exposure nor total privacy can carry collaboration.

**BOBA response:** Social Protocol of Angelic Outreach; braid-to-braid
coordination; enough disclosure to collaborate, enough boundary to preserve
authorship and consent.

**Open score:** what would you need to share to collaborate, and what would need
to remain held back?

**Sources:** `glossary-and-ontology.md`, `interagency-orientation-surface-v0.md`,
conversation `conversation-the-fog-the-braid-and-what-we-can-know-260706.md`.

## Chapter 12: The Collaborative Surface

**Human situation:** a book that cannot honestly end as a finished monument
because the thing it describes is a living collaboration.

**Pattern:** a system for Self-authorship should arrive as an invitation to
participate in its own becoming.

**BOBA response:** the book, site, Salon, repo, Asterisms, and future protocols
as overlapping surfaces.

**Open score:** how do you enter?

**Sources:** `book-as-collaborative-surface-score.md`, `book-publishing-plan.md`,
Salon docs, current BOBA website work.

## Epilogue: Eclosion

**Opening image:** the human bean sprout, ridiculous and sacred enough to keep.

**Pattern:** emergence is not finality. A life, a collaboration, and an
ecosystem can become more available to themselves.

**BOBA response:** invitation, not closure.

**Last note:** the door remains open.

## Running Interludes

The book should include short interludes between major movements:

- **Debugging In The Fog:** failed commands, hardware trouble, CUDA, WSL, and the
  comedy of trying to build clarity with brittle tools.
- **Bean Notes:** brief, playful returns to the human bean sprout image.
- **Weather Marks:** small dated records of what was known, unknown, and
  changing at the time.
- **Scores For The Reader:** simple practices that invite extrapolation without
  becoming homework.
- **Salon Invitations:** places where the reader can imagine answering back.

These interludes are how the book keeps its pores open.
