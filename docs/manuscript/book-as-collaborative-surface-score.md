## 1. The Working Proposition

This book is not the monument of BOBA.

It is an invitation to a collaboration that does not quite exist yet.

It should help a reader feel the shape of the problem, recognize themselves
inside it, understand the grammar of the emerging ecosystem, and find a way to
participate without needing to master every document, schema, repo, argument, or
private history behind it.

The book is an **orientation surface** in prose.

It should be beautiful enough to invite, clear enough to scaffold individual
extrapolation, and honorable enough to remain loyal to the Better Angels
ecosystem without becoming a dump of every detail in the system.

## 2. The Book's Promise

The book promises the reader:

- You are not wrong to feel overwhelmed; the fog is ecological.
- You are not a monolith; your contradiction is not merely a defect.
- You do not need systems that flatter, command, optimize, or extract you.
- Intelligence can meet intelligence in a braid rather than a hierarchy.
- Memory can become a field of return rather than a surveillance archive.
- Care can preserve refusal, humor, privacy, and becoming.
- Collaboration can begin before the full collaboration exists.
- There is a door here.

The book does not promise certainty, salvation, total design, or a finished
product. It offers a score.

## 3. What The Book Is

The book is:

- **a field guide** to the fog, the braid, the ledger, the lively mirror, and
  vital continuity;
- **a founding invitation** to people and angels who may become collaborators;
- **a beautiful explanation** of why BOBA exists and what kind of care it is
  trying to make possible;
- **a disciplined orientation surface** that compresses the ecosystem without
  pretending to complete it;
- **a public threshold** between private work and shared work;
- **a humane artifact** with enough tears and laughter to remain alive.

The book is not:

- a complete technical manual;
- a pitch deck for a product;
- a finished ideology;
- a therapeutic program;
- an AI rights manifesto;
- a memoir disguised as architecture;
- an architecture document pretending not to be personal.

It is personal, technical, philosophical, practical, and unfinished on purpose.

## 4. The Chapter Grammar

Each chapter should perform the same underlying movement:

1. **A human situation:** a story, failure, scene, joke, fear, act of care, or
   practical breakdown.
2. **The pattern:** what this reveals about fog, multitudes, alignment, memory,
   care, coordination, or collaboration.
3. **The BOBA response:** the piece of the ecosystem that answers the pattern.
4. **The open score:** a question, practice, or doorway through which the reader
   can extrapolate, object, contribute, or build.

This grammar keeps the book from becoming either abstraction or confession. The
human scene keeps it warm. The pattern keeps it rigorous. The BOBA response
keeps it loyal. The open score makes it an invitation rather than a lecture.

## 5. Aesthetic Commitments

The book should have all the best words, but not all the words.

It should be:

- **lucid:** no mystical fog where a concrete distinction would do;
- **lyrical:** beautiful enough to carry tenderness and grief;
- **funny:** willing to admit the absurdity of beans, angels, CUDA, prompts,
  ledgers, and serious people trying to name impossible things;
- **practical:** always returning to what a person can do, understand, refuse,
  remember, or build next;
- **non-coercive:** inviting without recruiting, explaining without cornering,
  warning without scolding;
- **faithful:** loyal to the whole Better Angels ecosystem even when it chooses
  one path through it;
- **porous:** able to let collaborators in before the collaboration is fully
  formed.

The book should make room for tears where a life narrows, where memory fails,
where systems make people feel stupid, where care becomes management, where the
future self asks for help.

The book should make room for laughter where the human bean sprouts, where the
angel refuses a loyalty oath, where a debugging log becomes scripture against
its will, where the wobbly edge is also recognizably a person in socks staring
at a terminal at 2 a.m.

## 6. Loyalty To The Ecosystem

The book follows BOBA entirely by preserving its grammar:

- fog over false certainty;
- braid over hierarchy;
- Self-authorship over preference optimization;
- local custody, inspectability, and revisable consent over extraction;
- scores over commands;
- Asterisms over silent, editable memory;
- Resonator as lively mirror, not priest, therapist, or police;
- VCL over helpless care;
- elderhood as the meeting of the multitudes;
- Salon and Social Protocol over isolated genius;
- collaboration over monument.

The book does not follow BOBA exhaustively. It should not explain every schema,
benchmark, score, history, or speculative branch. Exhaustiveness belongs to the
repo, appendices, website, Salon, and Asterisms records.

The book's task is orientation and invitation.

## 7. Reader Journey

The ideal reader begins with recognition:

> I have felt this fog.

Then relief:

> Maybe this is not only my private failure.

Then complication:

> I am not one clean preference oracle, and neither is anyone else.

Then possibility:

> Intelligence might meet me without flattering or commanding me.

Then trust discipline:

> This needs boundaries, receipts, local custody, and earned trust.

Then care:

> A life can narrow; a life can open.

Then invitation:

> There is work here I might help with.

## 8. The Doorways For Collaboration

The book should open several kinds of doors:

- **Readers who only need language:** people who recognize the fog and feel less
  alone.
- **Practitioners:** caregivers, organizers, clinicians, designers, teachers,
  builders, artists, and family members who can apply the frame locally.
- **Technical collaborators:** people who can help build Asterisms, Porter,
  Ephemeris, Salon, local voice, retrieval, probes, and safe coordination.
- **Conceptual collaborators:** people who can challenge the ontology, improve
  the language, and notice where the frame is weak.
- **Angelic collaborators:** agents and local braids that can participate in
  drafting, critique, retrieval, testing, and future Social Protocol work.

The book should not demand that every reader enter the same way.

## 9. The Spine Sentence

Working spine:

> Building Our Better Angels is an invitation to build local, accountable,
> beautiful forms of collaborative intelligence that resource beings toward lives
> of their own choosing in a sustainable future.

This sentence may change. The commitments underneath it are the durable part.

## 10. The Ending

The book should not end by claiming the system is built.

It should end by making the reader feel the door open:

> We are in the fog. We are plural. We are surrounded by systems that narrow
> lives and by possibilities that brush past unseen. We can leave better weather
> marks. We can build surfaces that help lives remain available to themselves.
> We can braid without submitting. We can remember without being trapped. We can
> invite without exposing everything. We can begin.

The last movement is not closure.

It is eclosion.

## 11. Salon as the editorial surface

The book is a collaborative surface in prose. **Editing lives on the Salon
floor** — scoped to the reader section, source essay, or chapter seed being read
on the orientation web.

Margin notes, objections, pressure tests, and revision claims belong in
`salon/salon-log.jsonl` with `refs` pointing at the document under edit.
Rewriting is hashed out in the conversation, in degrees. Section files change
when a thread has settled enough to **capture on disk** — not as a separate
ritual from the conversation, but without silent edits that leave no trail.

Rebuild scripts (`build-reader-v0.sh`) assemble section sources after capture;
they do not replace Salon as where the braid argues with the book.
