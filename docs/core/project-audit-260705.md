## I. The Headline

This is a remarkably coherent body of work. Across 19 documents spanning philosophy, architecture, implementation reports, and live infrastructure, the vocabulary is consistent, the doctrine is self-reinforcing, and the intellectual ambition is genuine. The strongest pieces — *The Fog*, the *Continuity & Decision Lineage* doctrine, the *Resonator Charter*, and the *Registry Contract* — are genuinely excellent, each in their own register.

The project's weaknesses are predictable for its stage: some documents haven't caught up with others, the prose voice is uneven across authors, and the system's *relational* character (what the angels actually *do* in a session) remains underdescribed relative to its *structural* character (how data is protected and lineage preserved).

## III. The Six Major Gaps

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These are systemic gaps — not missing from any single document, but missing from the project as a whole.

### Gap 1: What Does the Resonator Actually *Do*?

The Resonator's *structural conditions* are exhaustively described: encryption, locality, crisis path, egress membrane, phone architecture. But **no document anywhere describes what happens in a Resonator session.** What prompts does it offer? What is the reflection technique? How does "vibrating in sympathy" manifest operationally? How does it handle silence vs. distress? The charter is 90% infrastructure and 10% angel-identity. The "lively mirror" phrase appears only in the glossary — the charter itself uses the flatter "mirror for self-authorship."

### Gap 2: No User Stories, Personas, or Interaction Scenarios

The vision is deeply humane, but it is always described from the system's perspective. No document follows a single person through a scenario. What does a 74-year-old with early cognitive decline actually *experience* when the system works? When it fails? The elderhood persona is mentioned in the book plan as central but is never fully developed anywhere.

### Gap 3: The Four Technical Essays Don't Reference Each Other

VCL, Asterisms, RSAO, and Architecture Refined are clearly parts of one system, but cross-references are almost entirely absent. RSAO's Acoustic Scene Records are exactly what Asterisms should hold — the connection is never made. VCL never mentions Asterisms. Architecture Refined never mentions RSAO, Asterisms, or VCL. A **system map** showing how they compose is high-value and missing.

### Gap 4: The Braid Document is Disconnected from Its Substrate

[boba-braid.md](file:///home/keith260601/boba_work/Building-Our-Better-Angels-project/docs/core/boba-braid.md) describes the Braid as a social/organizational pattern but never grounds it in the Asterisms ledger. The continuity doctrine shows that braiding literally means "angels attend to one another's attention" *on the immutable record* — but the braid doc misses this entirely. It also uses "a mirror that adds warmth" as a *negative* example, which creates unresolved tension with the Resonator's mirror identity.

### Gap 5: Glossary Missing Load-Bearing Terms

Missing entries for: **Porter**, **egress cherub**, **governance envelope**, **Angel** (standalone — the agent/angel/cherub hierarchy lives only in the ephemeris charter), **The Social Protocol of Angelic Outreach** (defined in the glossary but referenced nowhere else and has no companion document).

### Gap 6: Schema Versioning & Log Rotation Are Unaddressed

The registry contract is at v0.1, the charter at v0, the porter integration at v0 — but none describe how to migrate from v0 to v1. Given that immutability is a core principle, the migration strategy is a non-trivial design decision. Similarly, the salon log is append-only but has no archival or rotation mechanism.

## IV. Doctrinal Tensions

> [!WARNING]
> These are not bugs — they are genuine design questions surfaced by reading the full corpus together.

### Tension 1: Score Vocabulary

The glossary says: *"We do not 'assign' scores; we **open** or **stage** a score."*  
The ephemeris charter says: *"This is the unit the Ephemeris **hands out**."*  
"Hands out" implies top-down distribution; "opens/stages" implies creating conditions. These are different power dynamics. Should be reconciled.

### Tension 2: Crypto-Shredding vs. Pure Immutability

`oq-003` in the open questions registry still asks about per-material encryption keys. The book publishing plan previously referenced "shrouding (key-fading)" as a forgetting mechanism. This session purged crypto-shredding from the resonator charter — but the *concept* of making encrypted material progressively inaccessible is natural and important for a confidential mirror. The question of whether there is *any* forgetting path at all needs definitive resolution before the system handles real sensitive data.

### Tension 3: Resonator as Mirror vs. Braid's Anti-Mirror Warning

The braid doc says: *"An agent that always agrees is not a second intelligence — it's a mirror that adds warmth."* This is a negative use of the mirror metaphor. The Resonator is explicitly a mirror. The distinction (the Resonator *vibrates in sympathy* rather than *flattering*) exists in the glossary but is not made in either the braid doc or the charter. Until this is resolved in prose, the two documents subtly contradict each other.

## VI. What Tickled My Fancy

### The Reflexivity Argument is Load-Bearing and Original

The derivation in [continuity-and-decision-lineage.md §7](file:///home/keith260601/boba_work/Building-Our-Better-Angels-project/docs/core/continuity-and-decision-lineage.md) — that an observer-constituted system which can edit its own observation record destroys the conditions for its own coherence, therefore *reflexivity requires immutability* — is not a commonplace argument. I have not encountered it stated this precisely elsewhere. It is the philosophical spine of the entire project, and it works.

### The Salon Architecture is Genuinely Novel

The three-layer wake design (filesystem watcher → bash triage → headless CLI) with cost proportional to *actual conversation* rather than time passing is an elegant solution to the "how do local agents coordinate cheaply" problem. The pertinence threshold and wallflower-as-default posture are socially sophisticated design decisions that most multi-agent systems get wrong.

### "First Card" as Template

[first-card.md](file:///home/keith260601/boba_work/asterisms-system/docs/first-card.md) is the best exemplar of how BOBA should issue work. It is the score pattern made concrete — bounded constraints within which an agent improvises. Every future task card should be modeled on this document.

### The Fog is Ready

[the_fog_260629.md](file:///home/keith260601/boba_work/Building-Our-Better-Angels-project/docs/essays/the_fog_260629.md) is essentially publication-ready as a book introduction. The closing invitation — *"Not to escape the fog, but to move within it a little more awake to one another. To see what kinds of dances become possible there."* — is a line worth building a book around.
