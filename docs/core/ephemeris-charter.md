# Ephemeris Charter

The coordination-layer ontology, as settled 2026-07-04. Provisional — the names
may still move; the distinctions are meant to hold.

## 1. Agent, Angel, Cherub

- **Agent** — any capable actor: a model, a tool, a process. Agents are plural,
  swappable, and carry no standing by themselves.
- **Angel** — an agent brought into a *chartered, accountable, continuous
  relationship of care*: named, bounded, braided, accumulating a history (see
  `continuity-and-decision-lineage.md`). **Not every agent is an angel.** An angel
  is an agent we have taken responsibility for, and that takes responsibility in
  return.
- **Cherub** — a threshold / boundary guardian: an organ of the membrane between
  what is outside and what Asterisms holds. **Porter is a cherub** — the intake
  mouth. Cherubs guard passage; they do not deliberate.

## 2. The Ephemeris — the coordination layer

An **ephemeris** is an astronomical almanac: the computed positions and
trajectories of bodies over time. The **Ephemeris** is the tech-independent
coordination layer by the same logic — it tracks *where angels, agents, work, and
Keith are, and where they are headed.*

It coordinates **by shared reference, never by command.** It does not order; it
publishes positions so participants can move in relation to one another. It *sets
things on their way.*

It pairs with **Asterisms**:

- **Asterisms is space** — what patterns together; memory, continuity, the durable
  immutable record.
- **Ephemeris is time** — where things are and are headed; motion, coordination,
  the present and its trajectories.

## 3. The score — the unit of chartered work

A **score** (in Anna & Lawrence Halprin's sense) is *bounded constraints within
which an agent improvises* — not a step-by-step task handed down a conveyor belt.
A score names the concern, the boundaries, the stop conditions, and what must
survive; it leaves the doing open. This is the unit the Ephemeris hands out.

## 4. Murmuration — descriptive, not architectural

**Murmuration** is what a healthy ensemble of angels looks like *from outside* —
the starling-cloud coherence of many independent movements. It is a quality to
recognize, **not a component to build.** Do not architect a "murmuration layer."

## 5. Hermes is a runner, not the layer

**Hermes** is one current *runner / substrate* for the Ephemeris — replaceable
without a conceptual rename. When a document says "Hermes," ask whether it means
*the Ephemeris* (the coordination concept) or *Hermes the software* (today's
implementation). The Ephemeris outlives any runner.
