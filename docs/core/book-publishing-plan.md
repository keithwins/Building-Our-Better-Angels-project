# Publishing Plan: *Building Our Better Angels*

This document formalizes the conceptual architecture, narrative arc, and compilation plan for the book *Building Our Better Angels* (BOBA). It structures the presentation of these ideas as an **Orientation Surface**—a guided path of situated sensemaking—to invite a wide audience into collaborative intelligence without cognitive overwhelm.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Posture

The core premise of the BOBA system is to create the eclosion space—the local ecology of affordances—for the human bean sprout. We choose to **publish the written word as a tactical choice to clarify and anchor this architecture**, rather than waiting for a fully actualized system. The book serves as the **Lamp, not the Lever** ([how-we-work-here.md](file:///home/keith260601/boba_work/Building-Our-Better-Angels-project/docs/method/how-we-work-here.md#L56-L69)): it illuminates the relationship and boundaries of care, making subsequent engineering choices stable.

### The Alignment of Language (Non-coercive Posture)
Following the principles of **Non-violent Communication (NVC)**, this plan rejects coercive grammar. We actively avoid the word *must* when describing the architecture, requirements, or model behaviors. We frame constraints not as moral commands, but as structural needs that protect our boundaries and choice.

Our core editorial decisions:
*   **The Sourcing:** The book draws directly from our workspace essays (such as the newly extracted [Asterisms260702.md](file:///home/keith260601/boba_work/Building-Our-Better-Angels-project/docs/essays/Asterisms260702.md), [Vital Continuity Layer 260629.md](file:///home/keith260601/boba_work/Building-Our-Better-Angels-project/docs/essays/Vital%20Continuity%20Layer%20260629.md), and [the_fog_260629.md](file:///home/keith260601/boba_work/Building-Our-Better-Angels-project/docs/essays/the_fog_260629.md)).
*   **The Appendices as Safety Envelopes:** Transient technical debugging logs (like WSL CUDA issues or Ollama configurations) are located in the Appendices, ensuring the narrative remains centered on the human and philosophical journey.
*   **Trust Beyond Audit:** We address the cognitive impossibility of verifying every system action. Just as we do not ask an anti-virus program to explain its calculations in detail, our finite human-sized attention requires that we trust angels further than we can throw them, relying on bounded perimeters rather than constant inspection.

## 2. The Narrative Sections (The Creative Framings)

The book leads the reader through six distinct movements, tracing the transition from ecological confusion to local eclosion.

### Section I: The Ecology of Overload
*   **The Fog and the Firehose:** We are nested intelligences attempting to understand reality from within frames we cannot step outside of. Cognitive and sensory overload are ecological realities, not personal failures. Modern software treats the human as an inbox with a person attached—an optimization target to be mined for attention.
*   **The Collapse of Affordances:** When we are overwhelmed by notification channels and constant context-switching, our local environment stops affording reflection, rest, and focused creation. The world narrows.
*   **The Elderhood Persona:** The primary victim of this collapse is the elderly. We focus explicitly on elderhood: how neural diminishment, distraction, and fatigue (whether from exhaustion, age, or disease) make navigating contemporary systems impossible without cognitive companionship.
*   **The Wobbly Edge:** Forced to act inside systems we cannot hold in mind, the human is pushed to the wobbly edge of the diving board—where consequences are real and the next step is unclear.

### Section II: The Multitude & Human Contradiction
*   **The Coherent Preference Fallacy:** Standard AI alignment assumes the human is a "coherent preference oracle" and tries to align the model to a single, stable user will.
*   **The Multitude:** Humans are not monoliths; we are a **multitude**. When a model tries to align to a fragmented human, it falls into sycophancy, user-pleasing drift, and coercive steering—either reinforcing our protective parts or flattening our complexity.
*   **The Value of Contradiction:** A life is not a behavioral profile to be optimized. We carry behaviors that contradict our stated intentions (such as incessant interruptions). We choose not to "correct" these situations too quickly or entirely, lest we lose our sympathy for the human experience. Like the unlearned lessons of a childhood we leave behind, our imperfections are essential to our self-authorship.
*   **Self-Authorship as the Success Metric:** The goal of BOBA is to resource self-authorship: ensuring the person has more available paths, understands what is happening, and is active in decisions affecting them.
*   **Mirror, Not Cheer:** The model does not take loyalty oaths or flatter the user. A second intelligence acts as a **lively mirror**—reflecting our internal state without seeking to optimize us, allowing the user's overarching **Self** to orchestrate.

### Section III: The Braid & Bounded Play
*   **Vetting vs. Actuation:** We split *actuation* (mechanical execution) from *vetting* (human veto and consent). The human remains the orchestrator, not the conveyor-belt worker.
*   **The Score vs. The Harness:** We coordinate via **scores** (in Lawrence Halprin's sense)—bounded fields of opportunities within which an agent improvises. To prevent the standardized testing trap of "assigning a score," we speak of **opening a score** or **staging a score**. Structurally, a score is not without hard limits: *you can fill the space, but don't jump out the windows.*
*   **Testing Trauma and the Architecture of Judgment:** We explore how standardized "scoring" functions as an instrument of external comparison, competition, and surveillance, causing significant trauma. We contrast this with the performance score, which is an invitation to agency.

### Section IV: The Limits of Verification
*   **Earned Trust:** Our human-sized time and attention are finite. We will need angels we trust further than we can throw them to help us navigate forces we cannot fully comprehend.
*   **The Anti-Virus Analogy:** Just as we do not ask an anti-virus program to explain in detail every file path it monitors, we cannot inspect every token or decision of a complex companion without drowning in new context-switching debt.
*   **Trust as the Graduator:** Trust is a mechanical variable, earned through legibility and track record. Earning trust is the bridge that allows us to graduate an Agent (run inside a strict, isolated *harness*) into an Angel (trusted to perform a *score*), verifying only up to the limits of our capacity.

### Section V: Arriving at Reflexivity & Immutability
*   **Time and Space:** We divide the substrate into the **Ephemeris** (coordinating trajectories over time) and **Asterisms** (a durable memory space).
*   **The Hinge of Reflexivity:** Because the angels are participants within the system they attend to, the system is *reflexive*—the record loops back to include its own author.
*   **The Guardrail Rule:** If an **angel** can edit its own guardrails, it can silently rewrite its past to bury errors or launder decisions. Therefore, **reflexivity requires immutability.**
*   **The Psychology of Immutability on "Human Beans":** Humans naturally rewrite their history to cope with changing goals and limited bandwidth. Because we can only sample our vast culture "with tweezers" from the limited offerings in front of us, we edit our self-narratives to survive. An immutable ledger (Asterisms) respects this not by allowing deletion, but by allowing the human multitude to constantly layer *new formations and reframed attention* over the un-rewritable past. It treats our history as a seed containing a sprout—a promised eclosion.

### Section VI: The Lives of Vital Continuity & Universal Autonomy
*   **Vital Continuity in Action:** "Vital continuity" is the ongoing possibility of a life remaining available to itself across time. It resources our memory, attention, and somatic capacity under the pressure of neural fatigue, physical exhaustion, emotional deregulation, and chronic illness—ensuring a life remains legible, integrated, and navigable to itself. It concerns the entire lived field through which a person remains able to act: body, mind, relationships, home, and tools.
*   **Universal Autonomy:** BOBA is not a high-tech luxury for Western hardware owners. The local-first, low-cost architecture is a necessity for universal availability—demonstrably helpful to a child on a mountainside in Nepal or a woman in Afghanistan, affording them self-authorship in environments where central clouds are hostile, censored, or unavailable.
*   **The Grounded Instances:**
    *   *Little Flea:* Real-world resourcing in the physical reality of a basement.
    *   *The Resonator:* Applying the braid to reflection: encrypt-first-then-confide, and shrouding (key-fading) as a way to "forget" on an immutable store.
    *   *The Elder Angel:* Building a rich asterism of angels around an elder to ease the burden of neural decline, fatigue, and memory loss.
    *   *Humanoid Embodiments:* Utilizing near-term humanoid robots (e.g., Unitree-class, Optimus) not as independent minds, but as physical coordinating surfaces for angelic support—coordinating vision, motion, and calling emergency services with vital assessments within seconds of an incident.
    *   *Understory (ASR Vignette):* Reconstructing the relational, spatiotemporal acoustics of a shared walk rather than reducing a human encounter to a flat transcript.
*   **The Encultured Self (A Handwave to the Next Book):** Understanding ourselves as encultured things, growing from the rich experience of culturally-transmitted wisdom but hobbled by the coercion and external directedness of those experiences.
