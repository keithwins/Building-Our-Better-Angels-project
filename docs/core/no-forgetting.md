# No Forgetting

**Adopted 2026-07-10.** Closes `oq-009` (and the related “forgetting path” /
crypto-shred / key-fading cluster formerly also gestured at as `oq-003`).

## Ruling

1. **No forgetting** on Asterisms, Ephemeris logs, or any durable BOBA substrate.
   True forgetting is historical rewriting by another door. It is not allowed.

2. **No crypto-shredding** — do not destroy encryption keys to make ciphertext
   permanently unrecoverable.

3. **No key-fading-to-oblivion** — do not design progressive key loss as a
   “forget on an immutable store” feature.

4. **“Shrouding” is retired** as a name for any forgetting path. Older prose that
   used shrouding/key-fading to mean forget is **superseded** by this ruling.

5. **Confidentiality ≠ forgetting.** Encrypt at rest, governance envelopes,
   egress stomata, access control, and invitation-spine allowlists constrain
   *who may read or surface what now*. They do not erase existence or lineage.

6. **Change of mind = new layer.** New formations, reframed attention, and
   supersession stubs over immutable past — never deletion of the past.
   (See Psychology of Immutability in the glossary; Continuity & Decision Lineage.)

7. **Immutable captures that argued otherwise** (notably the recovered Therapist
   Angel charter §7 crypto-shred text) remain in Asterisms as **superseded
   lineage**, not living design.

## What this is not

- Not a ban on the English word “forget” in ordinary prose or ML jargon
  (“catastrophic forgetting”).
- Not a ban on declining to *surface* sensitive material in a public invitation.
- Not a requirement that every byte be world-readable — only that recoverable
  history not be destroyed by design.
