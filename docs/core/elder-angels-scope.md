# Elder Angels Scoping Document

## Status

Elder Angels is the first concrete elderhood expression of the
[Vital Continuity Layer](../essays/Vital%20Continuity%20Layer%20260629.md).
It is where VCL stops being only a beautiful frame and becomes a working
practice: preserving and enlarging the real conditions through which an elder
can remain active in a life of their own.

The phrase "Elder Care" is useful as a bridge to the existing world, but it is
not the deepest frame. It can imply helplessness: an elder as an object of
management. VCL is the antidote to that reduction. The real frame is vital
continuity in elderhood: agency under changing conditions, held inside a wider
field of relationship, support, refusal, memory, pleasure, and possibility.

## What Is Elder Angels?

Elder Angels is a near-term BOBA deployment focused on an elderhood continuity
context: a specific person, a specific family or care circle, and a specific
field of services, histories, rooms, tools, obligations, opportunities, and
constraints. Its job is not to replace family judgment, clinical care, or human
companionship. Its job is to make the surrounding ecology of care more legible,
more navigable, and less dependent on exhausted people remembering everything at
once.

The core object is not a "case file." The core object is a living ecology of
affordances:

- the elder's own preferences, fears, rhythms, capacities, and inner multitude;
- family relationships, obligations, histories, limits, and gifts;
- clinicians, pharmacies, insurers, agencies, benefits, care workers, neighbors,
  transport, community groups, and local services;
- the home, tools, calendar, documents, medications, meals, mobility paths,
  hobbies, rituals, and ordinary rooms through which life is actually lived.

Elder Angels asks where that ecology is narrowing, where it could open, and what
small, consent-respecting intervention might create disproportionate practical
freedom. It is the meeting of the multitudes: the elder's inner plurality, the
family's plurality, and the wider social/service milieu becoming visible enough
to navigate.

## What Makes Elder Angels Distinct?

Elder Angels is not generic eldercare automation. It is distinct because:

- **It is person-centered without pretending the person is isolated.** Elderhood
  continuity always involves the meeting of multitudes: the elder's inner
  plurality, the family's plural needs, and the wider social/service milieu
  around them.
- **It treats care as affordance work.** A ride, a repaired hearing aid, a
  protected morning routine, a less confusing pillbox, or a neighborly check-in
  may matter as much as a medical recommendation.
- **It is consent-first and authority-bounded.** The system may observe,
  summarize, recommend, remind, notify, or coordinate only inside an explicit
  trust envelope.
- **It notices opportunity, not only risk.** The question is not merely "what
  might go wrong?" but "what viable path is passing unnoticed?"
- **It treats engagement as continuity work.** Meal choice, appointment
  preparation, medical history, family coordination, and autobiographical
  projects are strands in one living pattern, not separate task categories.
- **It keeps BOBA's non-coercive posture.** The goal is not compliance,
  institutional convenience, family control, or a helplessness script. The goal
  is vital continuity and Self-authorship under changing capacity.

## The Work It Actually Does

The first useful Elder Angels pass should be manual and small. It produces an
orientation surface, not an automated care regime.

Minimum pass:

1. Name the person or synthetic vignette being considered.
2. State the consent boundary and what facts are safe to use.
3. Map present affordances: what currently helps life remain available?
4. Map erosions: what is quietly making the world smaller?
5. Map hidden milieu: people, services, documents, tools, benefits, rooms,
   habits, and opportunities that may be relevant but are not currently visible
   enough.
6. Separate action classes:
   - suggestions the family can consider;
   - questions that need human confirmation;
   - reminders or logistics that could be delegated;
   - external contacts that require explicit approval;
   - medical/legal/financial matters that need qualified human authority.
7. Close with one or two reversible next steps, not a sprawling plan.

## Required Tools and Capabilities

Near-term tools can stay local and ordinary:

- **Care log:** a structured note of observations, questions, decisions, and
  follow-ups.
- **Opportunity board:** visible candidates for opening the ecology of
  affordances: rides, services, grants, home adaptations, companionship,
  activities, admin help, clinical questions, practical tools.
- **Boundary map:** who may know what, who may be contacted, who may act, and
  under what conditions.
- **Calendar/reminder layer:** appointments, medication-review prompts, recurring
  check-ins, renewal deadlines, and family coordination tasks.
- **Voice interface:** eventually primary for people who do not type easily, but
  only after the consent and privacy model is clear.
- **Porter/Asterisms seam:** private records enter through a guarded intake
  membrane; durable lineage and decision receipts land on the spine only at the
  appropriate resolution and sensitivity class.

Later capabilities may include local speech-to-text/text-to-speech, home
accessibility checklists, local service discovery, medication-coherence support,
caregiver load tracking, and emergency escalation. None of these belong in the
system before the consent and authority model is explicit.

## Privacy and Boundary Considerations

Elder Angels sits near sensitive health, family, financial, and emotional
information. The privacy posture is therefore part of the product, not a
compliance afterthought.

Operating boundaries:

- Do not ingest real elderhood/care details without Keith's explicit approval.
- Prefer synthetic or pseudonymized vignettes until the family consent posture is
  known.
- Keep medical, legal, and financial interpretation in an advisory/preparatory
  role unless a qualified human professional is explicitly involved.
- Treat family politics as data with consequences, not as gossip or color.
- Do not optimize for institutional convenience if it shrinks the elder's
  meaningful options.
- Preserve refusal, opacity, pleasure, risk, and eccentricity where they are part
  of the person's own life rather than preventable harm.
- Record what the system observed, inferred, recommended, and was authorized to
  do at a human-reviewable level.

## Open Keith Gates

The implementation should not cross these gates without Keith:

- whether to work from a real family situation, a pseudonymized situation, or a
  fictional composite;
- who the first care circle includes;
- what information may be remembered, summarized, or made durable;
- whether external services should be researched for a real locality;
- what communication channels, if any, can be used for family coordination;
- what "success" would feel like in the first month: less anxiety, more options,
  fewer missed details, better clinical preparation, more companionship, a safer
  home, or something else.

## First Concrete Next Step

Run one manual **Elderhood Milieu Pass** using either a synthetic elder or a
Keith-approved real/pseudonymized context. The artifact should be a one-page VCL
surface: current affordances, current erosions, hidden opportunities, boundary
questions, and one reversible next action.
