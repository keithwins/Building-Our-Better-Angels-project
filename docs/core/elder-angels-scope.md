# Elder Angels Scoping Document

## What is Elder Angels?
Elder Angels is a near-term, concrete BOBA deployment focused on eldercare for a specific family member. It represents a tangible step toward our broader mission of resourcing humans toward lives of their own choosing. While the core BOBA vision addresses systemic transformation, Elder Angels narrows the focus to immediate, actionable support for one family's unique situation.

## What Makes Elder Angels Distinct?
This initiative is characterized by its immediacy and concreteness. Unlike open-ended capability-building efforts, Elder Angels targets a specific, real-world scenario: supporting a particular family member's eldercare needs. The scope is bounded by the family's particular circumstances, making it a focused, actionable project rather than a broad philosophical exploration.
