# Engagement-Mediated Vital Continuity v0

This note names a sharper version of the Elder Angels/VCL hypothesis:

> Mental acuity, effective participation, and felt continuity are not only things
> we measure. They may be partly sustained by meaningful engagement with one's
> own life.

If that is true, the Angel's job is not merely to remind, schedule, monitor, or
summarize. Its job is to help the participant remain in contact with the living
pattern of their own life at a level they can welcome today.

This is not a pilot protocol. It is a design thesis and interaction frame.

## 1. The Principal Participant

The word "human" is too blunt here. It names a species, not the being whose
continuity the system is chartered to resource.

For now, use **principal participant** as a working phrase:

> The principal participant is the being whose vital continuity, agency, and
> Self-authorship the Angel is chartered to support.

This should not be hardened into ontology yet. The terminology has feathery
teeth: it needs to hold gently enough to keep thinking alive, but precisely
enough to prevent the system from quietly treating the participant as a patient,
case, resident, user, data source, or object of management.

The phrase also leaves open the larger question: whether some version of
Selfing, Self-authorship, or vital continuity applies across intelligences. That
question is real, but not needed for the elderhood pass to work.

## 2. The Core Design Claim

The system should not ask only:

- Did the participant eat?
- Did they take medication?
- Did they attend the appointment?
- Did they avoid falling?

It should also ask:

- Did the interaction help the participant come more awake to their own life?
- Did it make a meaningful choice easier?
- Did it reconnect a practical action to a valued identity, memory, project, or
  relationship?
- Did it preserve refusal, dignity, and authorship?
- Did it increase effective participation rather than merely compliance?

The deeper claim is that meal choice, appointments, autobiographical
design/build work, medical history, family memory, and daily logistics are not
separate domains. They are strands in one living continuity.

An Angel becomes useful when it can surface the right relation at the right
resolution.

## 3. Asterism Aflutter

An **asterism aflutter** is a timely constellation made available to the
participant.

It might connect:

- today's breakfast choice;
- yesterday's fatigue;
- the cardiology appointment next week;
- a favorite soup from childhood;
- the garden path that has become harder;
- a daughter's worry;
- an old workshop project;
- a medication question;
- a song that reliably reorients the morning.

The point is not maximal context. The point is a living relation offered at a
participant-sized moment.

Architecturally, this should be understood as an **orientation surface over
Asterisms**, not as the ledger itself becoming animated. Asterisms remains the
provenance spine. The aflutter surface is a temporary, consent-bound, energy-
appropriate presentation of selected relationships from memory, schedule,
context, and current state.

## 4. Interaction Has To Morph

The same participant needs different forms of invitation across time.

By energy:

- **High energy:** autobiographical design/build, planning, teaching,
  storytelling, taste, judgment, choice among real alternatives.
- **Medium energy:** appointment prep, meal choice, calendar review, simple
  project resumption, social invitations, "what matters today?"
- **Low energy:** one question, one memory, one song, one photo, one preserved
  thread for later.
- **Depleted/confused:** grounding, reassurance, orientation, no complex
  branching, no pressure to perform clarity.

By context:

- **Private:** identity, refusal, shame, pleasure, fear, memory, grief.
- **Family:** translation without disappearance: helping the care circle see the
  participant's context without replacing their voice.
- **Clinical:** preparation, symptom history, medication questions, observed
  changes, and explicit uncertainty.
- **Administrative:** forms, deadlines, bills, benefits, renewals, passwords,
  permissions.
- **Meaningful work/play:** making, gardening, music, repair, cooking, worship,
  study, conversation, contribution.
- **Crisis:** safety, escalation boundary, professional handoff, minimal prose.

The rule:

> Offer the richest interaction the participant can welcome today, not the
> richest interaction the system can generate.

## 5. Measures Of Welcome And Helpfulness

The system needs measures, but not all measures should be clinical.

Useful local signals:

- Does the participant return to the interaction voluntarily?
- Do they initiate more choices, questions, stories, corrections, or refusals?
- Do they participate more effectively in appointments or family conversations?
- Do they resume valued activities, even briefly?
- Do they show less dropped-thread anxiety because the Angel can hold continuity?
- Does the care circle report fewer missed details without feeling more
  controlling?
- Does the participant experience the system as invitation rather than
  surveillance?

Adjacent validated measures exist for cognition, daily function, quality of
life, mood, social participation, fatigue, pain, and caregiver burden. They are
useful if this becomes research. They do not directly measure BOBA's central
claim: whether a life is more available to itself.

That central claim needs a BOBA-local instrument, probably built around a few
repeated questions:

- I understand more of what is happening.
- I have options.
- This is still recognizably my life.
- I can refuse without being punished.
- I can pick up meaningful threads again.
- The people around me can help without taking over.

## 6. Failure Modes

This frame is powerful enough to be dangerous if mishandled.

- **Stimulation mistaken for engagement:** the system keeps things lively but
  exhausts the participant.
- **Compliance mistaken for agency:** the participant follows prompts but loses
  authorship.
- **Memory mistaken for continuity:** the system retrieves facts without
  restoring lived relation.
- **Family comfort mistaken for care:** relatives feel calmer because the system
  manages the elder, while the participant's option-space shrinks.
- **Over-personalization:** the Angel becomes too intimate too fast, before trust
  has been earned.
- **Ontology capture:** premature terms make the living being fit the system's
  categories.

The antidote is bounded invitation, explicit consent, reversible actions, and a
standing bias toward preserving refusal and surprise.

## 7. Readiness

Ready now:

- write synthetic interaction sketches;
- run manual Elderhood Milieu Passes;
- draft aflutter surfaces by hand;
- identify what an Angel should keep durable, hold only on ephemeral surfaces,
  or decline to surface publicly (never “forget” from the ledger —
  [`no-forgetting.md`](../core/no-forgetting.md));
- test whether the language produces useful next actions.

Not ready yet:

- resident deployment;
- clinical claims;
- autonomous intervention;
- real medical/family data in repo docs;
- facility studies or randomized trials.

The immediate next artifact is not a pilot. It is an interaction sketch vivid
enough to test whether the frame actually wakes up the design.
