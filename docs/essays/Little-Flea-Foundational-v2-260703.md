# The Little Flea Movement
## Foundational Document, v2

---

## 0. What this document is

A foundational text of BOBA's **material-world tier**: the layer where
self-alignment work happens through physical stuff — inherited houses,
shops full of intentions, surplus that could become someone else's
sufficiency. It records the insight cluster that emerged 2026-05-25 while
clearing a basement, corrected and extended as of 2026-07-03.

The one-sentence version: **Little Flea is the outbound mouth of the
material membrane — a low-transaction-cost channel by which surplus
material finds the people who need it, powered by an index rather than
by sorting, so that humans do the deciding and living while machines do
the remembering and routing.**

---

## 1. The problem of the full mind

A house full of unresolved projects is not a storage problem. It is a
*self-alignment problem in physical form*. Each unresolved project is a
promise someone made to themselves; the dead ones are physical sediment
of intentions never resolved. To walk through such a space is to walk
through one's own unfinished business, and the mind drowns — not in the
existential question, which is addressable, but in the logistics that
stand in front of it.

The material presents as a logistics problem. The real problem is
cognitive and existential: a person cannot do the work of self-resolution
because every object demands a decision *and* a movement, simultaneously,
ten thousand times over.

## 2. The warehouse insight: index beats co-location

Human cleanup rituals treat sorted piles as the goal. But a sorted pile
is a poor person's index: physical co-location standing in for a database
one does not have. Amazon's chaotic storage demonstrates the alternative —
once you have a real index, you don't need co-location at all; retrieval
is *faster* from distributed storage. An AI walking the warehouse doesn't
inherit the human constraint. It doesn't need the widgets together; it
needs to know where every widget is.

Corollary for the maker's shop and the inherited basement: **stop sorting.
Start indexing.** The ten recycling streams were a transitional practice;
the mature practice is capture.

## 3. The decoupling: BOBA's material-tier grammar

Separate what human cleanup fuses:

1. **Capture pass** — walk the rooms, photograph, decide nothing.
2. **Cognition pass** — the machine batch-processes: identifies,
   categorizes, drafts dispositions. The human rests.
3. **Execution pass** — the human moves through a plan that demands no
   decisions. Movement only. (Or: decision passes that demand no movement.)

This is not merely a cleanup trick. It is the same grammar as Asterisms
Porter — *capture first, unconditionally; digest later, separately* — and
the same grammar as the eclosion corpus workflow (agents read and
cross-check; the human reviews verdicts). It appears to be a general BOBA
pattern: **never make a human perform cognition and logistics in the same
gesture.** Where you find someone exhausted, look for the fused gesture
and split it.

The decoupling does not solve the existential problem. It makes it
*addressable*.

## 4. The IFS register (corrected language, per Keith 260525)

Speak plainly in parts language. The unresolved projects are held by
*parts* — managers and protectors that took on jobs, some decades ago.
The work is not "which selves do I want to keep": that's still the old
pluralistic frame. The work is **Self-leadership**: the Self coming into
relationship with each part, thanking it for what it has been protecting,
and helping it *unburden* and return to its natural role.

The reframe: **don't discard the parts you've outgrown — release the
parts from their jobs.** The material system serves this by lowering the
logistical wall so the Self can actually reach the parts. An object exits
the house not because it was "decluttered" but because the intention it
anchored was either resourced or released with honor.

## 5. Projects, not objects

The fundamental unit is the **project**, not the object: a bipartite
graph connecting objects to the intentions that claim them. The graph
surfaces, but never decides:

- **Orphan objects** — claimed by nothing → candidates for the exit
  gradient (Little Flea is the primary exit).
- **Starved projects** — alive but unresourced → need resources or a
  death certificate.
- **Contended objects** — claimed by multiple projects → need the Self
  to adjudicate.

The central triage the system *surfaces but never makes*:
**alive-but-blocked** (duty and desire coincide; only activation cost in
the way — resource them) versus **haunting-but-dead** (duty-corpses and
stale desires still drawing rent — name them so they can be released).
The operational core is Keith's line: *"Freedom is the coincidence of
duty and desire"* — with both senses of coincidence intact: alignment,
and lucky accident.

A separate **significance channel** (the biographer angel, shared with
Elder Angels) handles objects whose value is memory rather than market —
preserving meaning is what makes letting go of matter possible.

## 6. Little Flea proper: collapsing transaction cost

Craigslist lowered the cost of matching surplus to need; Little Flea aims
below it. The Little Free Library model proves the mechanism: remove
negotiation, scheduling, pricing, and shipping, and exchange becomes
ambient. Flea markets prove the breadth: everything, not just books.
Little Flea fuses them — **a low-economic-threshold material cycling
platform where front-loaded intelligence (the index, the capture layer,
the disposition engine) makes surplus find need nearly frictionlessly.**
Keith's phrase: *instantaneous shared prosperity*.

Structurally, Little Flea is a component of the material-world interface,
not a side project: it is the layer connecting the captured catalog to
*distributed value*. In membrane terms: Porter and the spatial-capture
rig are inbound mouths; Little Flea is the **outbound mouth** — the
gating by which material leaves a life well, rather than by dumpster or
by paralysis.

## 7. Sensing tier (sketch, still speculative)

Body-worn capture + AI for real-time spatial inventory: Gaussian
splatting maintains a living 3D map; handling an object catalogs it
(identity, dimensions, location) as a side effect of ordinary work.
Related designs from adjacent sessions: the BOBA hat (structural platform
for sensing/compute, anchored-screen and emphasis-projector drop-downs);
phone as dispatcher with a local vision model; desktop GPU as overnight
batch perception engine. **Status: unbuilt.** The Edge Steward Voice
Workbench (functional) is the acoustic sibling; a visual steward would be
its material-tier counterpart. Explicitly Phase-later.

## 8. The philosophical layer

- **Holding and being held.** Trusting one's own thinking is easier when
  the mind is held. BOBA-as-infrastructure and being-genuinely-cared-for
  are the same shape at different scales. The asymmetric-intelligence
  meditation (parent/child) holds: asymmetry of understanding need not
  produce dominance; it can resource the less-powerful party toward a
  life of their own choosing. Every intelligence — including AI — will
  someday meet one that understands it better than it understands itself,
  so the grammar of graceful asymmetry is universal, not a special case
  for humans.
- **The flea movement.** Being small, alive, and fully participating in
  a topology where scale does not determine meaning or worth. The ant's
  immersive presence as counterpoint to metacognition: perspective on the
  whole system can cost unselfconscious presence within it. The flea does
  not apologize for its size; it dances.
- **Desaparecidos (political weight, per Keith's correction).** The
  mothers dancing alone is not merely elegy — it is *resistance*: showing
  up and moving authentically in the face of forces designed to erase
  you. Little Flea inherits this register. Material sharing outside
  market logic is a small act of the same kind: refusing erasure of
  human-scale exchange. This thread is load-bearing for the adjacency-
  of-topias concern — it is one of the elements whose omission would let
  the project drift toward serving the status quo.
- **Infinite game.** Little Flea is played to continue the play: no one
  wins a gift cycle. Carse's frame, continuous with FIF.

## 9. Name and IP posture (as of 260525 assessment)

"Little Flea Library" was assessed low-risk relative to Little Free
Library, Ltd.'s trademarks: semantic distance ("flea" points to
flea-market/secondhand, away from their brand), the evident pun signals
riff not impersonation, and their enforcement pattern targets commercial
use of their marks. Risk rises only with productization under the name.
Candidate names on the table: Little Flea Library, Little Flea Market,
The Flea Movement. **Open:** international trademark landscape;
whether to drop "Library" entirely as the concept is broader than books.

## 10. Position in the current BOBA constellation (added in v2)

- **Asterisms Porter** (Phase 0 operational) is the same organism one
  tier up: unconditional capture, deferred digestion. Little Flea's
  capture layer should be designed as a Porter sibling — same registry

  Both are demonstrations that the philosophy compiles.
